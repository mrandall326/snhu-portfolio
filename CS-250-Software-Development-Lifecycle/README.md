# CS 250 – Software Development Lifecycle  
### Final Sprint Review & Retrospective

This repository contains my final project for **CS 250: Software Development Lifecycle** at Southern New Hampshire University.  
The primary artifact for this course is the **Final Sprint Review & Retrospective**, which reflects on the Agile processes, team collaboration, and development practices used throughout the simulated SNHU Travel project.

---

## 📌 Course Overview
CS 250 focuses on the principles and practices of the **Agile Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)**, including:

- Scrum roles, events, and artifacts  
- User stories and acceptance criteria  
- Sprint planning and estimation  
- Iterative development and continuous improvement  
- Professional communication within a development team  

The course emphasizes how Agile teams collaborate, adapt, and deliver value incrementally.

---

## 🧩 Final Sprint Review & Retrospective (Project Summary)
The final retrospective analyzes:

### **Sprint Review**
- What the team accomplished  
- How the product evolved during the sprint  
- How user stories and acceptance criteria were met  
- Feedback from stakeholders and opportunities for improvement  

### **Sprint Retrospective**
- What went well  
- What could be improved  
- How the team adapted its processes  
- Actionable steps for future sprints  

The document demonstrates an understanding of Agile values, transparency, and continuous improvement.
